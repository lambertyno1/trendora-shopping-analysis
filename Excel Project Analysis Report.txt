Excel Project Analysis Report
Project Title
Shopping Customer Data Analysis

Objective
The objective of this project was to clean and analyze customer shopping data using Microsoft Excel and generate useful business insights from the dataset.

Data Preparation
The dataset was cleaned and organized before analysis. The cleaned data contains customer information such as age group, gender, items purchased, category, purchase amount, location, season, rating, subscription status, shipping type, payment method, and discount value.

Analysis Performed
Pivot Tables were used to analyze:

Total purchase amount
Average customer rating
Number of unique customers
Top-performing products
Purchase amounts by age group
Purchases by season
Payment methods used by customers

Key Findings
Total purchase amount analyzed: $164,202
Average customer rating: 3.83
Total unique customers: 2,761
Dress generated the highest purchase amount among the top five items, at $7,403.
The 60–70 age group recorded the highest purchase amount at $38,959.
Fall had the highest share of purchases at approximately 26.1%.
Debit Card was the most frequently used payment method at approximately 35.6%.

Conclusion
The Excel analysis provided insights into customer purchasing patterns, product performance, age-group spending, seasonal purchasing, and payment preferences. These findings can support business decisions relating to products, customers, and sales strategies.
